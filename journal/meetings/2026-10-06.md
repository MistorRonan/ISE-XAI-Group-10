## Why

- **Dropping "political":** the week 2 source reviews didn't support it.
  - [Gururangan et al.](../sources/2026-10-01-gururangan-2022.md): the political correlation is confounded with rural geography, and the authors downplay it.
  - [Udagawa et al.](../sources/2026-10-05-udagawa-2025.md): the filter explicitly excludes political belief.
  - The week 2 recap had already flagged the political half of the title as "the weakest-supported".
- **Political terms also left out of Exp 2:** because of the title change (noted in the [EXP2 spec](../../experiments/identity%20term%20swap/EXP2_identity_swap.md)).
- **Dropping Integrated Gradients:** ___ *(Julia to add the reason. [Krishna et al.](../sources/2026-10-09-krishna-2022.md), reviewed the same week, shows IG agrees with gradient methods but not with LIME/SHAP.)*
- **Ideas A and B first:** both could be run on an off-the-shelf toxicity classifier (Detoxify) in time for the slides. Each tests a finding from our sources:
  - A tests the Mendu/Udagawa finding that reporting harm gets flagged.
  - B tests Udagawa's protected attributes and Gururangan's demographic findings.

  Idea C needed us to train our own filter first.
