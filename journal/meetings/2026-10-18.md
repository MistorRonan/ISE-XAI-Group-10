## Decisions Made

- **Thesis:** feature attribution is faithful enough to flag identity-term bias in a pretraining filter, but not reliable enough to certify the filter is fair.
- **Paper plan:** [2026-10-18-paper-plan.md](2026-10-18-paper-plan.md). IEEE two-column format, 3 pages of body + references on their own page (to be confirmed with the lecturer).
