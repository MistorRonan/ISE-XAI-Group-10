## Decisions Made

- Topic description:

    - Working title: Explaining the Gatekeepers: Using Feature Attribution to Audit Political and Social Bias in LLM Pretraining Data Filters
    - Summary: 
    Large language models are trained on web-scale corpora (e.g. Common Crawl) that are cleaned before training. Pipelines such as C4, Dolma and FineWeb remove "toxic", "extremist" or "low-quality" text. They do this with keyword blocklists, toxicity classifiers (e.g. Perspective API, Detoxify) and learned quality classifiers. These filters are rarely explained or audited, yet they decide which voices, dialects and political viewpoints the model learns from. Prior work shows the harm. Blocklist filtering in C4 disproportionately removed text by and about LGBTQ+ people and in African-American English. Quality filters favour text from wealthier, more educated sources. Hate-speech classifiers show racial bias.
    
        Our group examines how post-hoc explainability methods can reveal which features drive a filter's removal decisions. We focus on feature attribution: SHAP, LIME and Integrated Gradients. We then ask whether those explanations are trustworthy enough to serve as an auditing tool.
