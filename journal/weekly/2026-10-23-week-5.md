## What changed our minds

> [!WARNING]
> **Before:** The presentation told auditors to "use SHAP/LIME disagreement as a flag for human review" and "report LIME's R²".
> **After:** Disagreement found SHAP's worst errors no better than chance (AUC 0.46). Low R² did a bit better (0.64). Weak signal strength was the best warning sign (AUC 0.80).
> **Because:** Exp 6 tested these recommendations directly against Exp 3's counterfactual error. The [EXP6 spec](../../experiments/lime%20stability/EXP6_lime_stability.md) said in advance that we would drop or soften them if they failed, so we did. The Discussion now argues that agreement is not validation (Section V-C).

> [!WARNING]
> **Before:** We expected LIME to be least stable on weak signals.
> **After:** Instability across seeds is unrelated to signal strength (|ρ| < 0.05). What does drop on weak signals is how well LIME's surrogate fits: mean R² is 0.41 on the weaker half of sentences against 0.77 on the stronger half.
> **Because:** LIME can give the same answer every run and still be a poor fit. Being stable doesn't make it right, which made the Discussion's point stronger.

> [!WARNING]
> **Before:** The identity word's share of SHAP attribution rose from 46% to 60% under `unbiased`, which looked like the "debiased" model relies *more* on identity.
> **After:** Its measured counterfactual effect rose by only 0.23 log-odds, and SHAP's estimate of that effect actually fell.
> **Because:** Exp 3 gave us a reference to check the attribution summary against. Without that reference, we would have reported something misleading. This became Discussion V-A.

> [!WARNING]
> **Before:** We read the deletion test (Exp 5) as a straight measure of explanation quality.
> **After:** Sufficiency came out *negative* (the top words alone scored as more toxic than the full sentence), most strongly in report sentences (−3.4).
> **Because:** Deleting the context turns a sentence that reports abuse into a bare insult. Such fragments are out of distribution for the filter ([Hase et al. 2021](../sources/2026-10-22-hase-2021.md)), so deletion metrics partly measure how the filter reacts to fragments. This became Discussion V-B.
