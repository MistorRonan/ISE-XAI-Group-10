## Common threads across this week's sources

- **Filters encode values.** "Quality", "toxic" and "negative regard" are all defined by someone's reference corpus, taxonomy or LLM labeller (Gururangan, Mendu, Udagawa). None of them is neutral ground truth.
- **Nobody explains individual removals.** All three filter papers audit at the aggregate level (regression, prevalence statistics, word co-occurrence). None shows *which words drove a specific decision*, which is exactly what our attribution approach adds.
- **Reporting about harm gets caught.** Both Mendu (reporting pages as false positives) and Udagawa (a news report of a racist attack being downsampled) show filters struggling to tell *discussing* harm apart from *doing* harm.
- **Accuracy ≠ explainability.** Ngueajio's survey backs up evaluating explanations separately (faithfulness, plausibility) rather than just reporting F1.
- **The "political" half of our title is the weakest-supported.** Gururangan's political signal is confounded and Udagawa excludes politics. Only Mendu's Ideological Harm category and Ngueajio's citation of Wich et al. touch on it directly.
